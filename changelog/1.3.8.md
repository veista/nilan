# 1.3.8 (10/08/2026)

**Title:** Nordic HVAC: Auto/Off only (heat/cool are status)

**Tag:** [v1.3.8](https://github.com/master3395/veista-nilan/releases/tag/v1.3.8)  
**Author:** master3395

## What changed

- **CTS700_NORDIC** climate no longer offers Heat/Cool as selectable modes (same class of issue as DHW Off)
- Holding **5432** remains a status read: heating/cooling still appear as HVAC *action*
- Selectable modes: **Auto** and **Off**; comfort via room setpoint and fan step
- Turn-on from Off writes Nordic mode **3** (auto/dehum path) instead of forcing heat
