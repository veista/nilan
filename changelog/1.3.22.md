## What changed

- **Panel operating mode** sensor from holding **5432** with panel-matching labels: Off / Cooling / Heating / Dehumidification / Hot water production
- Nordic **control_state** now maps active DHW (`5432=4` and compressor effort **4706 > 0**) to state **9** (Hot water production). Previously DHW collapsed to Ventilation, so water heaters never saw “heating” from control state
- Friendlier Hot water production naming on control_state state 9 (EN/NB/DE/FI/IT)
- Docs + FAQ: effort (4706) is not duty type; 5432 is; CTS602 priority **1702** illegal on Nordic; **20184** readable but unverified

## Operator notes

- Live read-only probe (slave 1): `5432=3` (dehum), `4706=0`, CTS602 `1002`/`1702` illegal address, `20184=1`
- Full DHW vs air-heat correlation still needs a heating+DHW demand event with effort > 0
- Room climate stays Auto/Off; do not use 5432 as a writable heat/cool setpoint
